## Execution and accounting

The original paper adapter still rejects live endpoints. The new live adapter uses separate live keys and the official US Trading API endpoint, validates the exact account, ACTIVE/USD status and restriction flags, and permits no mutation in read-only mode. Automatic SDK HTTP-status retries are disabled for live clients; uncertain submissions recover by the original stable ID.

BUYs use IOC limits capped five basis points above a freshly revalidated ask, with tick rounding downward and no market fallback. Sizing cannot exceed the configured capital, absolute order cap, account cash constraints, cash reserve, remaining exposure and original plan. Live entries check tradable US-equity eligibility and regular-session buffers. Read-only preflight checks completed-bar coverage, feed access and whole-share affordability; current asks are used when the market is open.

New live entries receive confirmed protection before another planned entry. Pending acceptance is checked with bounded retries under stable IDs. Unconfirmed protection latches a halt and attempts the existing risk-exit path without relying on another historical-bar download. That path retains cancellation confirmation, raced-fill reconciliation, retry bounds and stable IDs; an unavailable broker remains an explicit error. Broker-generated replacement stop IDs retain ownership through the journal. External SELL orders are not silently canceled.

Saved executions now preserve decimal broker quantities and cumulative cursors. Identity changes, overfills, corrected completed protective executions and inconsistent cursors require reconciliation before further trading. Fractions, shorts, external holdings and possible corporate-action share changes have structured diagnostic codes and review actions. Splits, mergers, symbol changes and unsupported fractional exits are not fabricated into agreement.

Absolute total/daily euro loss observations persist independently of the ordinary percentage drawdown latch. Overnight observed losses count against the new day's daily budget. Deleted risk files cannot rebaseline previously initialized accounts or completed trading histories. Non-trade activities distinguish external cash flows from dividends/fees; corrected, missing or unsupported activities block new entries. Baseline initialization samples activity stability around the account-equity read. These polling snapshots are not audited returns and cannot eliminate delayed activity publication; funding must settle before start and external changes require stopping/review.

A dated ECB reference supplies informational EUR/USD marking, with verified TLS, bounded downloads, atomic cache, freshness checks and no FX trading. Stale FX or unavailable cash-activity risk views block entries while known-position USD exits/protection remain permitted when account and holdings validation succeeds. The separate health file exposes those warnings. Intraday FX, gaps and unavailable execution can exceed the stated loss triggers; no loss guarantee is claimed.
