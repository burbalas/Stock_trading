# Usability and execution review — October 21, 2026

## Outcome

The project now has a desktop application and a supervised background paper operator. The manager and worker were checked after the launching terminal exited; both remained active. The first two current-candidate paper entries filled, their protective stops were confirmed at the broker, and reconciliation passed.

At the start of this review the account was still flat at $99,440.16 and the last actual session heartbeat was September 15. No candidate forward-trading sample had accumulated during the intervening month. The new operation starts that evidence collection; it does not establish a profitable strategy.

## Changes

- Added a responsive Tkinter window with start/stop, buy pause/resume, broker refresh, positions, stop coverage, logs and activity reports. All network/process work runs off the UI thread. Opening the window alone does not start trades.
- Added a hidden background manager, bounded worker recovery, persistent user stop state, and optional Windows sign-in resume. The desktop window can close while the operator continues. Windows Store Python and terminal process-lifetime behavior were exercised with real detached-process probes.
- Migrated authoritative runtime files outside OneDrive under locks and verified their hashes. Original source files remain intact. Canonical redirects preserve existing CLI compatibility, including Windows Store AppData aliases. Credentials and research datasets were excluded.
- Added a user-wide submitting lock across journal directories and rejected unexpected account changes before synchronization. Saved fills, reservations and drawdown history are preserved.
- Added explicit five-second connect and thirty-second socket-read timeouts to pooled Alpaca SDK sessions. Failed account refreshes retain the last complete view instead of clearing positions midway through an error. Closed-market polling reuses its clock client instead of repeatedly fetching unrelated account/position data.
- Revalidates each BUY immediately before reservation/submission: current account, held positions, fresh executable ask, drawdown, daily limits, position slots, cooldown, cash reserve and size caps. The quantity can decrease but cannot exceed the original plan. Market-order execution can still move beyond the sampled ask.
- Isolates initial quote failures to the affected BUY. A wide AMD quote uncovered during the connected trial previously stopped the entire signal scan. It now rejects that candidate while preserving valid candidates and exits, keeping the existing two-percent spread and two-minute quote-age limits.
- Rechecks Stop and Pause buys after broker/quote requests and immediately before each BUY reservation. A control change during a slow request rejects the pending entry without creating an intent; exits and protection remain available.
- Generates offline activity reports, separates preview/account/parameter cohorts, and records observations without claiming cash-flow-adjusted performance or attributing old fills to the new candidate.

The broker then confirmed active, unfilled SELL stop orders for exactly those quantities (AMD $498.40 and V $366.55 at the recorded check). At approximately 22:05 EEST the two positions reconciled, both had confirmed stop coverage, and no uncovered position was reported. Cash was $80,268.80; equity moved with the market. Intraday positive or negative marks over these minutes are not evidence of strategy quality. Stop orders do not guarantee execution at their trigger prices.

Windows sign-in startup remains **off** unless selected in the desktop app. A nonexecutable temporary probe confirmed that this Store Python can write the actual per-user Startup folder; the probe was removed and no startup entry was installed by the review.

## What remains before real money

The adapter remains paper-only. Fractional residual fills and broker corporate actions still need a complete holdings/accounting policy. Market-entry price limits and realistic fees/cash flows need validation, and the user's intended funded capital and acceptable loss are unspecified. Reliable supervision also requires an awake, connected machine; this local app is not a cloud deployment.

The frozen candidate still lacks meaningful forward results and has not established a net advantage over passive alternatives. Continue observing unchanged rules, execution and drawdowns rather than selecting another historical winner after each loss. Alpaca itself identifies differences between simulated and real execution in its [paper-trading documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading).

The desktop shortcut and [app guide](APP_GUIDE.md) are the primary operating entry points. Existing local source changes remain uncommitted.
